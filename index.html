4R

Recall. Reveal. Rate. Repeat.

A simple flashcard method for learning and remembering vocabulary. Recall the answer, reveal the card, rate yourself, and repeat what needs more practice.

The 4R Method

Four simple steps to turn practice into lasting vocabulary.

01

Recall

Look at the word and try to remember its meaning before revealing the answer.

02

Reveal

Flip the card to reveal the answer and check how well you remembered it.

03

Rate

Mark the word as known or unknown based on your answer.

04

Repeat

Focus on the words that need more practice and repeat until they stick.
